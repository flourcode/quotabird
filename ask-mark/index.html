Questions

Is this really Mark?
No, it's an AI set up to answer the way Mark would, built from what he's written and how he thinks about deals, partners, quota and comp. It's blunt because he is. It's not him, it doesn't know you, and it forgets this conversation when you close the tab. The real Mark is on the button at the bottom.
What can I ask?
Anything about selling, especially federal cloud and SaaS: a stuck deal, a prime that won't commit, a vehicle you're not on, a champion who can't sign, co-sell that goes nowhere, a commit that isn't burning, a quota that just landed, a comp plan with footnotes, a boss who wants it in commit. Commercial deals too. When the question is really arithmetic, it hands you to the QuotaBird tool that runs your numbers.
Can I trust it?
Like advice from someone experienced who wasn't in the room. It knows how federal deals actually move and it'll tell you what to do this week. It doesn't know your customer's org chart or this year's budget line, so check any name, vehicle or number before you repeat it to a customer. When it doesn't know, it should say so.
Does anything I type leave my device?
Yes. This page uses AI. When you press Ask, your messages go to Google's Gemini through QuotaBird's own server, and the answer comes back here. The server doesn't save them, and the real Mark never sees them. The conversation lives in this tab: it survives a reload or a trip to one of the tools and back, and it's gone when you close the tab. Don't paste anything that isn't yours to share: nothing classified or controlled unclassified, nothing marked internal, nothing about a customer you wouldn't put in an email to a stranger.
