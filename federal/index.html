Federal GTM

You think you have a federal business. Let's find out.

For companies deciding whether federal is real for them, and for people who need an honest read on a federal business somebody else says is real.

A federal customer liking your product isn't the same as a federal customer with money, a legal way to buy it, and a reason to do it this year. Most federal plans I've seen were built on the first part and assumed the rest. Hope is not evidence.

I spent 20 years as a Marine officer, some of it on the government side of technology buying, and later led Federal Partner Sales at Amazon across Defense, Federal Civilian, Federal Financial and National Security. I've watched this get bought from both sides of the table.

Who this is for

  • Startups thinking about federal, and commercial technology companies moving into the public sector
  • SaaS companies getting pulled into federal by a customer
  • Companies trying to figure out partners, primes and integrators
  • Sales leaders inheriting a federal business, or making their first federal sales hire
  • Investors and acquirers who need to know whether a federal pipeline is real

Federal GTM Pressure Test

$600

A working session for a founder, CRO or sales leader. We go through actual customer demand, funding, the buying path, partners, product readiness, sales coverage, your current pipeline and timing, and what should happen over the next 6 to 12 months.

  • A 90-minute working session.

An easy first step, not a consulting proposal.

Federal GTM Sprint

Starting at $2,500

For a company that needs more than a conversation. A short, finite engagement that ends with a pressure test and a working plan: which customers and accounts to start with, how they can buy, the likely contract vehicles, a partner and prime strategy, what the federal fiscal year means for your timing, a pipeline reality check, the profile of your first federal hire, first-year priorities, and the risks and assumptions underneath all of it.

  • A working plan you can run, with the assumptions written down.
  • A pressure test of the plan before you spend money on it.

A working plan, not a 75-slide strategy deck.

Federal Revenue Reality Check

Starting at $3,500

For investors, acquirers or executives who need an experienced outside read on a federal business. Is the pipeline real, and how old are the opportunities? Who's the incumbent? How much depends on one partner or one contract vehicle? What has actually been won, where is it concentrated, and who walks out the door with the relationships? What's coming up for recompete, and how much of the claimed traction is funded work?

  • A scoped review of the pipeline, history and dependencies you can share.
  • A plain written read on what holds up and what doesn't.
