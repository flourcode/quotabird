Questions

What can I ask?
Anything a QuotaBird tool can answer: whether a quota is crazy, whether your pipeline covers the number, what a comp plan pays, whether a deal is real, whether a rep or a territory is the problem, and the rest of the tools. It can also start you on Meeting Prep, Account Plan or Update Upward. It isn't a general chatbot. Ask it about the weather and it'll tell you so.
Does this use AI?
Yes, this page does, and it's the only kind of page on QuotaBird that does. What you type goes to Google's Gemini, through QuotaBird's own server, to work out which check you need and to explain the result. The math itself still runs here in your browser, with the same code the tool pages use. The server doesn't save what you type, and QuotaBird never sees it. Leave out names you wouldn't put in an email to a stranger.
Does the AI do the math?
No. The AI reads what you wrote and pulls out the numbers it finds there, quoting your own words for each one, and you can see and change every number before anything runs. Then the tool's own code runs on this page, the same code as the tool page. The AI only explains the result it was handed, and the server deletes any figure in the explanation that didn't come from you or from the math.
Why not just use the tool?
If you know which one you need, use it. It's faster, and nothing leaves your device. This page is for when you don't know where to start, or you want the result explained in plain words.
