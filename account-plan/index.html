Questions

Where does the math come from?
It runs on this page, in plain arithmetic: your open pipeline against the number, the coverage your win rate needs (1 divided by the win rate, the same as Pipeline Check), the gap, and each deal placed in the customer's fiscal quarter by its close month. The AI gets those numbers and isn't allowed to change them.
Can I trust what it says about the customer?
Only the parts marked as yours. Everything in the answer carries a label: what you said, what came from a document you pasted, what QuotaBird calculated, and what is a guess. A guess is a starting point for a question, never a fact about the customer. It won't name people, budgets, contracts or deadlines you didn't give it, and if it does by mistake, the server drops that line before you see it.
Does anything I type leave my device?
Yes. Unlike the tools, this page uses AI. When you press the button, what you typed goes to Google's Gemini, through QuotaBird's own server, and the answer comes back here. The server doesn't save it, and QuotaBird never sees it. The arithmetic runs here in your browser, not in the AI. Don't paste anything that isn't yours to share: no controlled unclassified information, nothing marked internal, nothing from a customer you wouldn't put in an email to a stranger.
Why the customer's fiscal year?
Because that's when their money moves. A federal customer's year starts October 1, and a lot of the year's buying lands in July through September. A plan on your calendar instead of theirs puts the work in the wrong months.
