Questions

What is this?
Free sales tools from Mark Flournoy, who led federal partner sales teams at Amazon. Tell the AI version of Mark what's going on and it'll tell you what he'd do. When the question is really arithmetic, the calculators run your numbers in your browser. The federal-specific ones say so.
Is any of this AI?
The calculators aren't. They're questions and arithmetic, so the same inputs always give the same answer, and they show the math. Your forecast has enough imagination in it already. The chat on this page is AI, and so are Ask Mark, Meeting Prep, Account Plan, Update Upward, Partner Check and Federal Readiness Check. Each one says so, and the real Mark is a click away.
Does anything I enter leave my device?
Not from the calculators. They run in your browser: no account, no CRM connection, nothing sent to a server. What you type into an AI page goes to Google's Gemini through QuotaBird's server to get an answer, and isn't stored. Google Analytics counts page views but never sees your numbers. The details are on the privacy page.
Is there a Claude plugin?
Yes. Search for QuotaBird in Claude's plugin directory. It runs thirteen of these checks inside Claude and works alongside Anthropic's Sales plugin.
What does it cost?
Nothing. The first conversation with the real Mark is free too.
