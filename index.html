How these work

Most take about a minute. Nothing you type goes into a system your boss can see.

There's no login and no AI. The checks ask the annoying questions a good VP is going to ask anyway, and then give you a straight answer and something to go do. The calculators just do the math.

I'm still adding to it. It's all free, so use whatever helps.

Questions

Who is this for?
Sellers and sales managers who carry a number, mostly in cloud, AI, cyber and data, mostly selling to government. Most of the tools work the same anywhere; the federal-specific ones say so.
Does anything I enter leave my device?
No. Everything runs in your browser, so there's no account, no CRM connection and nothing sent to a server. I use Google Analytics to count page views, but it never sees your answers or your numbers. Nothing leaves the page unless you share a result.
Is any of this AI?
No. It's questions and arithmetic, so the same inputs always give the same answer, and it shows the math. Your forecast has enough imagination in it already.
What does it cost?
The tools are free. If you want to talk something through, the first conversation is free too.
