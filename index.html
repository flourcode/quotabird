Questions

What is this?
A practical sales site for people who carry a number, built by Mark Flournoy, who led federal partner sales teams at Amazon. Tell the AI version of Mark what's going on and he'll tell you what he thinks. When the question is really arithmetic, the calculators and checks run your actual numbers in your browser. Most of it works the same anywhere; the federal-specific tools, like Deal Check, say so.
Does anything I enter leave my device?
Not from the tools. They run in your browser, so there's no account, no CRM connection and nothing sent to a server. The four AI pages (Ask Mark, Meeting Prep, Account Plan, Update Upward) are the exception, and say so on the page. QuotaBird uses Google Analytics to count page views, but it never sees your answers or your numbers. Base, variable and quota can be remembered in your own browser so the next tool fills them in. Nothing leaves the page unless you share a result. The details are on the privacy page.
Is any of this AI?
The tools aren't. They're questions and arithmetic, so the same inputs always give the same answer, and they show the math. Your forecast has enough imagination in it already. Four pages do use AI: Ask Mark, Meeting Prep, Account Plan and Update Upward, where an AI version of Mark answers questions about your deal. It says it's AI, and the real Mark is a click away. The calculators stay plain math.
Is there a Claude plugin?
Yes. Search for QuotaBird in Claude's plugin directory. It runs thirteen of these checks inside Claude, from quota and pipeline to comp plans and offers, and it works alongside Anthropic's Sales plugin: that one runs the sales day from your CRM, and QuotaBird checks the number.
What does it cost?
The tools are free. If you want to talk something through, the first conversation is free too.
